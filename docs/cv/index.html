    Bernardo Stearns¶

    Research Machine Learning Engineer & PhD Candidate (NLP)

    Google Scholar · LinkedIn · Based in Ireland · Dual citizen: Luxembourg (EU) & Brazil

    Profile

    Research machine learning engineer implementing frontier NLP research models into industry partners. I design rigorous, reproducible experiments, pre-train and fine-tune language models at scale, and own the full path from research idea to deployed pipeline. My PhD and three EU Horizon 2020 research projects run on the same craft: adapting state-of-the-art models to hard, low-resource problems and engineering them into production with human-in-the-loop feedback.

    Research Experience¶

    Insight Centre for Data Analytics — Galway, Ireland¶

    Project Lead & Research Machine Learning Engineer · Jan 2024–Present

    • Leading the development of NLP pipelines to automatically generate and assess aviation exam questions from educational content.
    • Designed end-to-end workflows including model development, Dockerized deployment, and interactive dashboards.
    • Drafted project proposals and internal documentation (IDFs); project fully funded for extension due to client satisfaction.
    • Applied transformer-based models to assess question quality, ensuring reproducibility and scalability.

    Senior Research Machine Learning Engineer · Jun 2019–Dec 2023

    • Core developer on two EU research projects: Cardamom — closing the resource gap for minority and under-resourced languages through deep-learning NLP and cross-lingual transfer — and Prêt-à-LLOD — increasing language-technology uptake by combining linked data with containerized, readily deployable NLP services.
    • Investigated how modern transformer models adapt to low-resource settings: built standardized, reproducible evaluation pipelines and benchmarked fine-tuning strategies (full, domain-adaptive, and parameter-efficient). Stack: PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers, Weights & Biases.
    • Designed and built Teanga, a framework that composes containerized REST APIs into orchestrated NLP workflows — automated service-compatibility matching from OpenAPI specs, a visual workflow builder, and a CLI. Stack: Apache Airflow, Docker, OpenAPI, FastAPI.
    • Mentored MSc students and junior researchers in experimental design, model evaluation, and production-readiness practices.

    Research Machine Learning Engineer · Dec 2017–Jun 2019

    • Built high-performance ML models for financial opinion extraction and text summarization.
    • Standardized reproducible experimentation pipelines; applied deep learning and NLP techniques (PyTorch, SpaCy, FastAI).
    • Contributed to multiple publications and research reports.
